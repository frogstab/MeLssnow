# 2010年英二图表作文

## Directions

In this section, you are asked to write an essay based on the following chart. In your writing, you should

1) interpret the chart, and
2) give your comments.

You should write at least 150 words.
Write your essay on the ANSWER SHEET. (15 points)

![](https://origin.picgo.net/2026/10/05/6406a5a7e9f1eb8e7e8.webp)

## Article

The above bar chart clearly reveals the markedly different growth rates of cell phone subscriptions between wealthy nations and emerging economies. As is shown in the chart, the number of handset subscribers in advanced countries experienced a steady and slight increase from 2000 to 2007 and remained constant since then. In sharp contrast, there was a progressive rise in the number of wireless phone users in developing world from 2000 to 2004, followed by a steep growth from 2004 to 2008

This trend rests with a host of contributing forces. For one thing, rich countries are typically in possession of sophisticated technologies, empowering them to popularize cellular phones first. However,thanks to the sharing of cutting-edge technologies and the huge improvement in people's living standards,growing numbers of individuals in less developed areas are capable of employing various hand-held phones conveniently. For another, industrial nations have a relatively sparse population while the bulk of underdeveloped countries are populous. Therefore,the handset market in affluent nations is quite mature after many years of growth. But with the slipping prices of mobile phones, the majority of folks in developing countries have access to this service.

As is discussed above, it is not surprising to cast a glance over this chart again. And it can be concludedthat developing countries are quickly catching up with developed nations in science and technology. Thistide, in my eyes, is tremendously beneficial to bridging the gap between them in many spheres.

# 2011 年英二 图表作文

## Directions
Write an essay based on the following chart. In your writing, you should
1) interpret the chart and
2) give your comments.
You should write at least 150 words.
Write your essay on ANSWER SHEET. (15 points)

![](https://origin.picgo.net/2026/10/05/640954c03f7ca091291.webp)

## Article

The bar chart above demonstrates clearly that some changes have taken place in terms of the car marketin China. Based upon the data of the chart, it can be seen that the sales percentage of domestic cars was on arise, while the percentage of cars made in Japan witnessed a decline. In the year of 2008, the market share ofthe Chinese cars was nearly 25%, while within no more than a year, it went up to nearly 33%. The Japanese cars occupied 35% of China's car market in 2008, while the percentage went down to 25% in 2009.

It's of no difficulty for us to come up with some possible factors to account for this trend. At the top ofthe list, with the rapid development of economy and society, more and more people prefer domestic products to foreign ones. It is reported that approximately 55% of families in Chinese big cities are fond of cars madein China. In addition, we must admit that the government has made great efforts to appeal to us to support our native industries. Last but not least, the fact cannot be ignored that the cars made in China are increasingly having better quality.

Taking into account what has been agreed on, we can come to the conclusion that this established trend is positive and therefore acceptable. And I'm firmly certain that this trend will continue in the years to come.

## Article

By drawing this table, the author intends to visually reveal the results of a recently-conducted survey with respect to the employment satisfaction of staff among three age groups. As is shown in the above table,only 16.7% of employees aged 40 and under are satisfied with their work. In the group aged between 41 and50, all the workers either harbor unclear views or feel dissatisfied with their jobs. In the over-50 agecategory, staff who are content constitute a larger proportion than that of another two groups, accounting for 40.0%.

Job satisfaction refers to the degree of pleasure employees can feel when working. And the disparity presented in the table can be boiled down to their different attitudes. As to the employees aged 40 or under,they have not yet cultivated a sense of identity about their posts, or failed to gain job continuity and security.In terms of those middle-aged workers, unfavorable reality has piled up enormous pressure on them. Theyhave to assume the responsibility to feed the whole family, both the children and the elderly. Therefore, they cannot derive much gratification from their positions. For those senior workers, they have created a positiveself-image and nurtured an optimistic outlook about life or work, and more importantly, their contribution shave been valued and appreciated. Thus, they are much more inclined to obtain a higher level of jobsatisfaction.

Employment satisfaction is of broad interest to employers owing to its potential impacts on productivity and morale of employees. Therefore, it is fairly advisable for employers to frame a string of policies to satisfy the reasonable needs of employees and mobilize their enthusiasm,which is undoubtedly hugely rewarding for the long-term and sustainable development of businesses.
